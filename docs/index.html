SAJDA

Know what prayer is next and how long you have left — from your menu bar. The panel turns red when prayer is imminent and the adhan plays when it begins. Times calculated on your Mac, never sent anywhere.

get it

Download

Requires macOS Sonoma 14.0+. Signed with Developer ID, notarized by Apple, self-updating.

What it does

Next prayer, always visible

Icon, countdown, exact time, or icon + time — your choice of menu bar style.

Red alert

The panel goes red a set number of minutes before prayer. You choose how early.

Adhan audio

Five bundled public-domain adhan recordings, per-prayer choice, mute toggles per row.

Mosque timetables

Import an annual PDF or monthly CSV and show the mosque's own adhan, iqama and Jumu'ah times — fully offline.

Calculation methods

Several methods, madhhab selection, high-latitude rules, per-prayer time correction.

Nine languages

English, Arabic (RTL, Eastern numerals), Indonesian, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese.

Privacy

  • No accounts, analytics, or tracking. The app has no servers of its own.
  • Prayer times are calculated offline with the Adhan library; location stays on your device.
  • City search sends only the text you type to OpenStreetMap Nominatim — identified as Sajda.
  • Settings live in your local UserDefaults. Nothing else leaves your Mac.

open source

The whole app is MIT-licensed and on GitHub — read it, build it, fork it. Acknowledgements for every bundled library, sound and icon live in the app's About page.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. MIT-licensed, open source, no paywalls. Ko-fi and GitHub Sponsors links exist if you want to support development anyway.

Does it need my location?

Automatic location is one option — you can also pick a city by name or enter coordinates manually. Times are computed on-device either way.

What are mosque timetables?

Import the calendar your mosque publishes (PDF or CSV) and Sajda shows its adhan times, its iqama gaps, and its Jumu'ah sessions instead of calculated ones. Stored locally, works offline.

Where does it phone home?

Only to OpenStreetMap for city search (when you type one), and to GitHub for update checks. No analytics, no crash reporters, no accounts.
