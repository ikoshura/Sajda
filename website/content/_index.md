---
title: Sajda Pro
description: A minimalist prayer times app for the macOS menu bar. Next-prayer countdown, red alert, mosque timetables — offline, no tracking, no accounts.
---

Sajda lives in your menu bar and tells you what matters: **what prayer is next, and how long you have**. It turns red when prayer is imminent, plays the adhan when it starts, and calculates your times offline with the Adhan library — no account, no analytics, no tracking.
